AbMaxxing support

How can we help?

Find quick answers below or email us. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened so we can help faster. Please do not email sensitive health information or personal photos.

Subscriptions & purchases

Open AbMaxxing and use Restore Purchases if an active subscription is not recognized. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your App Store account settings.

Camera & photo access

Camera and photo access are optional and used only when you choose photo-assisted scan or meal features. You can change access in iOS Settings at any time.

AI-assisted estimates

Core scans and meal estimates are informational tools. Review nutrition estimates before saving. Core scan results are not medical advice, a diagnosis, or a measure of health.

Privacy & deletion

Read the Privacy Policy for details about processing, retention, and your rights. Most workout, scan, meal, and weight history is stored on your device.

Contact support

Email deepanshu07s@gmail.com. We aim to respond as soon as practical. For billing refunds, use Apple’s Report a Problem service.
